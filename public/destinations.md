# Destinations & Geographic Footprint — Rupkotha Travels

Rupkotha Travels operates journeys across three distinct geographic zones in India, Bhutan, and Nepal.

---

## Zone 1: Alpine Peaks, High Passes & Glacial Valleys (Himalayan Range)

- **Ladakh**: Leh Palace, Pangong Tso, Nubra Valley sand dunes, Khardung La (17,982 ft), Siachen Base Camp, Hanle Dark Sky Reserve, Tso Moriri.
- **Spiti Valley & Kinnaur (Himachal Pradesh)**: Sangla, Kalpa, Tabo 1000-year monastery, Dhankar, Kaza, Langza, Hikkim, Komic, Chandratal Lake, Kunzum Pass.
- **Sandakphu & Singalila (West Bengal/Sikkim Border)**: Manebhanjan, Tonglu, Tumling, Kalipokhri, Sandakphu summit (11,930 ft), Phalut, views of Everest & Kanchenjunga.
- **Sikkim & North Bengal**: Gangtok, Gurudongmar Lake, Yumthang Valley, Pelling, Ravangla, Darjeeling, Lava, Rishyap, Lolegaon.
- **Bhutan**: Thimphu, Punakha Dzong, Paro Tiger's Nest Monastery (Taktsang), Phobjikha Valley.

---

## Zone 2: Wildlife Sanctuaries & Conservation Reserves

- **Tadoba-Andhari Tiger Reserve (Maharashtra)**: Moharli, Kolara, Navegaon gates. Top density of Royal Bengal Tigers in central India.
- **Kaziranga National Park (Assam)**: Central Kohora, Bagori & Agaratoli ranges. One-horned Rhinoceros, wild water buffalo, elephant herds.
- **Eco Tour Odisha Sanctuaries**: Similipal Tiger Reserve, Debrigarh Wildlife Sanctuary on Hirakud Reservoir, Satkosia Gorge on Mahanadi River, Bhitarkanika Mangrove & saltwater crocodiles, Mangalajodi wetland birds on Chilika Lake.
- **Kanger Valley National Park (Chhattisgarh)**: Kutumsar limestone caves, Dandak caves, Tirathgarh waterfall, Bastar hill myna habitat.
- **Sundarbans Biosphere (West Bengal)**: Sajnekhali, Sudhanyakhali, Dobanki canopy walk, Netidhopani, estuarine crocodile & mangrove biodiversity.

---

## Zone 3: Cultural Heritage, Sacred Confluences & Coasts

- **Odisha Coastal & Tribal Corridors**: Puri Jagannath Temple, Konark Sun Temple, Raghurajpur heritage craft village, Chilika Lake, Koraput tribal belt, Duduma waterfalls.
- **Bastar & Chhattisgarh Heritage**: Jagdalpur, Chitrakote Horseshoe Falls, Danteshwari Temple, Kondagaon bell-metal craft, Sirpur archaeological complex.
- **Rajasthan Royal Forts**: Jaipur, Ajmer, Pushkar, Chittorgarh, Udaipur, Mount Abu, Jodhpur, Bikaner, Jaisalmer Golden Fort & Thar sand dunes.
- **Andaman & Nicobar Islands**: Port Blair Cellular Jail, Havelock Island (Radhanagar & Elephant Beach), Neil Island (Bharatpur & Laxmanpur Beach), Baratang limestone caves.
- **Kerala Backwaters & Hills**: Munnar tea hills, Thekkady spice plantations, Alleppey private houseboat cruises, Kovalam beach.
