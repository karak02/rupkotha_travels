# Tour Categories — Rupkotha Travels

Rupkotha Travels structures its expeditions across three core operational types:

---

## Type 1: Fixed Group Departures & Cultural Heritage
- **Operational Focus**: Pre-scheduled escorted group tours departing from Kolkata with Sleeper class train tickets from Howrah and Sealdah.
- **Key Inclusions**: Train berths, dedicated MUV / Tempo ground transit, verified hotel/camp stays, 4-course daily homely meals (bed tea, breakfast, lunch, evening tea & snacks, dinner), and station luggage porters.
- **Representative Circuits**: Rajasthan Grand Heritage (13 Days), Koraput & Chitrakote Tribal Tour (8 Days), Bastar Dussehra (8 Days), Hornbill Festival Nagaland (7 Days).
- **Target Audience**: Families, seniors, and travelers seeking seamless logistics and hassle-free escorted journeys.

---

## Type 2: High-Altitude Himalayan Expeditions
- **Operational Focus**: Trans-Himalayan overland journeys, high mountain passes, off-road 4x4 tracks, and ridge treks.
- **Key Inclusions**: 4x4 Scorpio/Bolero/Innova vehicles, oxygen cylinders, pulse oximeters, inner-line permits, altitude acclimatization pauses, and high-altitude mountain drivers.
- **Representative Circuits**: Spiti Valley & Chandratal (10 Days), Sandakphu Singalila Ridge Trek (7 Days), Ladakh Siachen Base Camp & Hanle (LAD 01–08), North Sikkim & Gurudongmar.
- **Target Audience**: Adventure photographers, nature enthusiasts, and high-altitude road trippers.

---

## Type 3: Eco-Forest Safaris & Coastal Getaways
- **Operational Focus**: Wildlife conservation reserves, core tiger game safaris, pristine river sanctuaries, and tropical island waters.
- **Key Inclusions**: Core and buffer safari permits, open 4x4 forest gypsies, certified forest naturalists, premium forest rest houses / eco-resorts, and boat cruises.
- **Representative Circuits**: Tadoba Tiger Reserve 4-Safari Expedition (6 Days), Sundarbans Mangrove Delta Cruise (3 Days), Eco Tour Odisha Nature Camps (Debrigarh, Satkosia, Similipal), Andaman Coral Islands (5–7 Days).
- **Target Audience**: Wildlife photographers, birdwatchers, conservationists, and coastal holidaymakers.
