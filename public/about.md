# About Rupkotha Travels (রূপকথা ট্রাভেলস)

**Organization Name**: Rupkotha Travels (রূপকথা ট্রাভেলস)  
**Location**: Kolkata, West Bengal, India (Railway Corridor Hub near Howrah & Sealdah)  
**Official Authorizations**: Authorized Booking Agent for Eco Tour Odisha & Chhattisgarh Tourism Board  
**Specialties**: Escorted Small-Group Fixed Departures, High-Altitude Himalayan Expeditions, Eco-Wildlife Safaris, Coastal Getaways

---

## Executive Summary

Rupkotha Travels is an experiential tour operator founded in West Bengal, delivering fully coordinated, escorted journeys across India, Bhutan, and Nepal. The agency combines transparent all-inclusive pricing with confirmed train bookings from Kolkata, handpicked accommodations, dedicated MUV/Tempo vehicle fleets, and authentic 4-course daily meals tailored for conscious travelers.

---

## Official Certifications & Authority

1. **Eco Tour Odisha Authorized Agent**: Direct reservation access to government-managed community eco-camps across Similipal, Debrigarh, Satkosia Gorge, Daringbadi, Mangalajodi, and Chandaka.
2. **Chhattisgarh Tourism Board Booking Counter**: Authorized facilitation for tribal eco-tourism in Bastar, Chitrakote Falls, Kanger Valley National Park, and Sirpur heritage corridors.

---

## Core Travel Pillars

- **End-to-End Train Logistics**: Pre-reserved Non-AC Sleeper or 3AC train journeys originating from Howrah and Sealdah stations, complete with luggage porter assistance at major hubs.
- **Dedicated Ground Fleet**: Rigorously maintained vehicles (Scorpio, Bolero, Innova, Xylo, 13-Seater and 17-Seater Tempo Travellers) driven by veteran high-altitude and forest-certified drivers.
- **Homely 4-Meal Plan**: Daily morning bed tea, hearty breakfast, full 4-course lunch (veg & non-veg options with fish/chicken/egg), evening tea with snacks, and wholesome dinner.
- **Escorted Group Directors**: Experienced Bengali-speaking tour escorts who manage ground logistics, hotel check-ins, medical emergencies, and local sightseeing coordination.
