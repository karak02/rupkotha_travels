# Booking & Reservation Desk — Rupkotha Travels

How to reserve fixed departures, customize private circuits, and book authorized eco-tours.

---

## 4-Step Booking Process

1. **Select Package or Circuit**: Browse fixed departure dates or request a customized itinerary for Ladakh, Andaman, Odisha, or Spiti.
2. **Submit Traveler Details**: Provide names, age, food preferences (veg/non-veg), and boarding point (Howrah, Sealdah, Kolkata, or on-route).
3. **Pay Advance Deposit**: Secure your train berth and hotel rooms with an initial booking token (usually 30% to 50% depending on seasonality).
4. **Receive Confirmed Tour Voucher**: Receive detailed itinerary, confirmed train PNR status, packing recommendations, and Tour Manager emergency contact numbers.
