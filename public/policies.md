# Policies, Inclusions & Cancellation Rules — Rupkotha Travels

Transparent travel conditions for all fixed departures and custom packages.

---

## What Is Included in Fixed Departures

1. **Train Tickets**: Non-AC 3-Tier Sleeper or Non-AC Chair Car train journey from/to Sealdah / Howrah / Kolkata stations (except Ladakh, Zanskar, and Hornbill tours where flights or specified transit apply). 3AC upgrades available on request based on IRCTC fare differences.
2. **Ground Transportation**: Dedicated MUVs (Scorpio, Bolero, Innova, Xylo: 6–7 guests/car) or Tempo Travellers (13s: 9–10 guests, 17s: 12–14 guests).
3. **Accommodation**: Double-bedded or Triple-bedded (subject to availability) clean hotel rooms or safari camps with attached modern washrooms.
4. **4-Meal Catering Plan**: Daily Bed Tea, hearty Breakfast, 4-course Lunch, Evening High Tea with hot snacks, and Dinner. Wholesome Bengali and Indian preparations using fresh local ingredients.
5. **Luggage Porterage**: Station porter charges included at Howrah/Sealdah and major transit rail stations.
6. **Tour Escort**: Dedicated tour director accompanying the group from start to finish.

---

## What Is Excluded

- Personal expenses, laundry, telephone calls, camera/video camera entrance fees at monuments.
- Room service charges, packaged mineral water bottles, and cool drinks.
- Train food/meals during transit (unless explicitly mentioned).
- Any extra expenses arising from train/flight delays, strikes, landslides, bad weather, or natural calamities.

---

## Cancellation & Refund Tier Schedule

Cancellation notices must be submitted in writing or via official email/WhatsApp:

| Notice Period Before Departure | Deduction Amount | Refund Amount |
|---|---|---|
| **60 Days or More** | 10% of total package price | 90% Refund |
| **45 to 59 Days** | 30% of total package price | 70% Refund |
| **30 to 44 Days** | 40% of total package price | 60% Refund |
| **15 to 29 Days** | 60% of total package price | 40% Refund |
| **Less than 15 Days / No Show** | 100% of package price | No Refund |

---

## Child Fare Policy

- **Children under 5 Years**: Free (sharing parent's bed, no separate vehicle seat or train berth).
- **Children aged 6 to 11 Years**: Discounted Child Fare applies (includes separate vehicle seat, hotel extra mattress/bed, and child meal plan).
- **12 Years & Above**: Charged at standard adult rate.
